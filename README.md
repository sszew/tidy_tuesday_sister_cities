# Tidy Tuesday: Sister Cities (May 12, 2026)

For #TidyTuesday this week, we are looking at global sister cities.



"Sister cities" are a form of legal or social agreement between two geographically and politically distinct localities for the purpose of promoting cultural and commercial ties." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sister_city



Have a look at the interactive map to see how your city is connected to other cities across the world!
